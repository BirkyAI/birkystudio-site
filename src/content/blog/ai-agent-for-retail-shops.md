# AI Agent for Retail: Nobody Answers at 7pm

It is 7:40 on a Thursday evening in November. A woman stops in front of your shop window on her way home, checks the hours on the door, and calls to ask whether you have the green coat in a medium. The phone rings four times and goes to voicemail. She does not leave a message. On the bus home she finds the same coat online, pays for it, and it arrives in two days.

You will never know she called. You will never know she stood outside your window for thirty seconds.

Retail owners worry about foot traffic and window displays, but the sale rarely dies in the window. It dies in the ninety seconds nobody could pick up the phone, and in the message that arrived while you were serving the person in front of you.

## This is not really a retail problem

Agents describe the exact same shape in the real estate threads, in a line that has stayed with me: *"Biggest drop-off isn't lead capture, it's the gap between 'someone showed interest' and 'someone is actually booked in'."*

Swap the nouns and it is your shop. Someone showed interest. Nobody answered them. And in the last six weeks of the year, more people show interest at once than in the whole rest of the quarter.

## The six weeks that decide the year

| Phase | What walks in the door | What usually happens | What it costs |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Mid October | Early gift research, first "do you have this" calls | The phone rings while someone restocks the rail | One unanswered call, one lost customer list |
| Late October | Sizing and colour questions, hold requests | DMs sit unread until after closing | The item sells to somebody else |
| Early November | Bulk and corporate gift enquiries | One person on the floor, three channels live | The big order goes to whoever replies first |
| Black Friday week | Peak volume, holiday hours | Every available hand is with a customer | The phone becomes a wall of voicemail |
| December | Shipping panic, "is it in stock" | The same four questions, forty times a day | Staff answer and serve nobody |
| Late December | Exchanges, "in another size", gift receipts | Nothing gets written down | Patterns never surface for next year |

Read the middle column again. Nobody decided any of that. It is the arithmetic of a small team in a season that asks for a big one.

## The note nobody wrote

Underneath the missed calls is a quieter leak. Someone messages at 9pm: "I need a medium in the green, I'll come Saturday." It gets answered in the morning with a thumbs up, and then it lives inside a message thread nobody opens again. Saturday arrives, the coat is still on the rail, the customer is not, and by Monday the hold has quietly expired.

That is not a discipline problem, it is a records problem. The detail existed for exactly as long as the scroll back.

## What the agent actually does in a shop

- Answers calls, texts, WhatsApp, and Instagram DMs around the clock, including the hours after you close and the Mondays you are shut.
- Handles the questions your staff repeat all day: opening hours, location, price, sizes still available, delivery, and the returns policy.
- Captures the real request and the contact details, so a "do you have" becomes a name and a number instead of a dead call.
- Puts a hold or a reservation in the calendar and reminds the customer, instead of hoping they come back.
- Books a fitting or a collection slot, with a reminder, so the appointment does not evaporate.
- Follows up on unclaimed holds on day one, day three, and day seven. Not yet is not no.
- Keeps one running record per customer: what they wanted, what they were told, what is still open.
- Flags anything a human should handle, a complaint, a large order, an odd request, and hands it over with the context attached.

None of that replaces anyone behind the counter. It covers the ninety seconds when nobody is at the phone.

## What it will not do

It will not sell a product you do not have, and it will not conjure customers off an empty street. It cannot chase a channel it was never connected to, and it should never pretend to be you. If trade is slow because the market is slow, no agent fixes that, and I will not pretend otherwise.

I will also not hand you a number. Nobody measured your shop. If a vendor quotes you "thirty percent more sales this quarter," ask them which shop they measured and how.
