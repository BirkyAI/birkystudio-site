A 58 year old agent moving into a new market posted this on r/RealEstateTechnology last week:

> "Then I'm told it takes 10 hours to edit and you really should not edit your own videos. Now it's a very expensive coach program where they edit them all for you. I'm torn. UGH."

If you have ever tried to put a listing on video, you have felt that exact UGH. You buy the camera, you shoot the walkthrough, and then the ground opens up. Someone tells you it is ten hours of editing, that you are not the right person to do it, and conveniently, the only remedy costs a monthly subscription.

Here is the part nobody shows you: the ten hours is real, but it is not editing.

## Where the ten hours actually goes

I accounted for one listing walkthrough video the way it really happens for an agent who is also running a business. This is not a studio shoot. It is an agent, a phone on a gimbal, and a laptop at the kitchen table that night.

| The job | Rough time |
|:--|:--|
| Write the walkthrough outline and the opening line | 45 min |
| Shot list, pack the car, set up, light the rooms | 45 min |
| Filming the actual walkthrough | 60 min |
| The rough cut: trim, sequence, soundtrack | 90 min |
| Captions, plus a written description for the MLS | 45 min |
| Rewriting that description for Zillow, Facebook, and Instagram | 60 min |
| Thumbnail, title, posting, tags, scheduling | 45 min |
| Answering the comments and DMs, then following up | 60 min |
| Re-uploading because one platform rejected the file | 30 min |

Call it ten hours. Now read the right column again. The camera work, the shot list, and the cut together are about three of those hours. The other seven are words, formatting, and follow-up. In other words, the same work you already do for every listing, just wearing a video costume.

If this feels familiar, it is because it is the same story as [one property becoming five separate jobs](https://birkystudio.com/blog/one-property-five-jobs/). Crop the photos, rewrite the copy for each platform, check the safe zones, export to the MLS, track what performed. Add a camera and you do not get a sixth job. You get all five again, this time with a soundtrack.

## The upsell hides in the wrong hour

Watch how the advice is shaped. The moment editing gets defined as the ten hour problem, the answer is obvious: buy editing. Priced by the month, forever, plus a course to get in.

But a video editor cannot touch hours one through three, or the description rewrites, or the follow-up. The editor fixes the cut and the other seven hours stay exactly where they were, standing in your kitchen at 10pm. You have paid a subscription to remove the smallest line in the table.

## Which hours an AI agent actually deletes

A Birky Studio agent cannot hold a camera, and it should never pretend to. What it can do is take the hours around the camera:

- It drafts the walkthrough outline and the opening line from the property details and your notes, so you start from a draft instead of a blank page. That is the same [one step removed](https://birkystudio.com/blog/the-ai-you-forget-youre-using/) that makes any tool worth keeping.
- It writes the listing description once and reformats it for the MLS, Zillow, Facebook, and Instagram without you retyping anything.
- It answers the comments and DMs on day one, then follows up with everyone who engaged on day three and day seven, which is the hour that most often gets skipped when the week gets busy.
- It keeps every version of the description and every reply in one running note against the property, so the next listing starts from a draft you can improve, not from scratch.

The honest version: the cut still takes the ninety minutes, and it should, because that is the part viewers actually notice. Hours one, two, part of five, and most of six, seven, and eight disappear.

## What we will not pretend

- **If you do not want to be on camera, that is a legitimate business decision, not a gap we can sell you out of.** Plenty of agents close plenty of deals without filming a single walkthrough. The agent works your calls, texts, WhatsApp, and email whether or not you ever pick up a camera, which is why the honest sales pitch is never "you must make video."
- **An AI agent will not make a boring walkthrough good.** Filming is a craft. You still decide whether the property is worth the camera.
- **I am not going to invent a percentage.** I cannot tell you this cuts your production time by 47%, because I did not measure your videos and neither did anyone quoting a number like that. I can tell you exactly which rows leave the table above. Count them yourself.
