# How a Local Dentist Booked 18 New Patients in One Week with an AI Text Receptionist

**A $297 one-time setup. Seven days. Eighteen new patient appointments.**

If I told you that for less than the cost of a weekend in Tikal you could add a full-time employee who answers every text, WhatsApp message, and web chat inquiry — never sleeps, never takes a day off, and speaks fluent English and Spanish — you would probably ask what the catch is.

There is no catch. Here is exactly how it happened for one dental practice, the numbers behind it, and why asking "does it work?" is asking the wrong question.

## The Problem: Leads That Vanished Into Thin Air

A family dentistry practice in Guatemala City came to us with a frustration I hear from nearly every small business owner I talk to.

They were running Facebook and Google ads. They were getting leads — people filling out contact forms, sending WhatsApp messages, asking about teeth whitening and braces and emergency appointments. But by the time the front desk opened at 8 AM the next morning, half those leads had gone cold.

Here is what their lead data looked like before the AI text receptionist:

| Time of Inquiry | What Happened | Result |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 8:02 PM — WhatsApp: "How much for a cleaning?" | Nobody saw it until next morning | Lost — booked elsewhere |
| 11:30 PM — Web form: "Emergency tooth pain" | Email arrived at 8 AM, 30 min late | Lost — went to another clinic |
| Saturday 3 PM — Text: "Do you do Invisalign?" | Read Monday morning | Lost — lost interest over weekend |
| Sunday 9 AM — WhatsApp voice note | Nobody listening | Not even listened to |

Over the course of a month, they counted **37 leads that made contact but never booked**. Each one was a potential new patient. Each one had a lifetime value of $1,200–$2,400 in recurring dental work.

The front desk was doing great work during business hours. But the business was operating on a 9-to-5 schedule while its customers were inquiring 24/7.

## The Solution: An AI Text Receptionist That Answers in Seconds

We set up an AI text receptionist — a Birky Studio AI Agent configured specifically for dental intake — connected to their WhatsApp Business number and embedded as a chat widget on their website.

The cost: **$297 one-time setup, $0 per month** for the agent itself (usage-based WhatsApp API fees apply, typically $10–$20/month depending on volume).

Here is what the AI handles automatically:

- **New patient inquiries** — asks the right questions (name, insurance, reason for visit)
- **Appointment scheduling** — checks the calendar, books slots, sends confirmation texts

The receptionist (human) still handles walk-ins, phone calls during business hours, and complex insurance cases. The AI handles everything that comes in through text, WhatsApp, and the website — the channels where the leads were bleeding out.

## Why This Works for Text (Not Just Voice)

A lot of business owners ask us about voice receptionists first, and we love our [AI voice receptionist](/blog/ai-voice-receptionist-books-clients-while-you-sleep/) — it is a fantastic product. But for many businesses, **text is actually the higher-converting channel**.

Here is why:

1. **Text is low-friction.** Sending a WhatsApp message takes five seconds. Calling a business takes courage and time. People procrastinate on calls. They act immediately on texts.

2. **Text is permanent.** A phone conversation disappears into memory. A WhatsApp chat stays in the phone — the patient can re-read the appointment time, the address, the price.

3. **Text works while you sleep.** The AI responds instantly at 2 AM. The patient wakes up with a confirmed appointment on their calendar, not a voicemail notification.

4. **Text is preferred by younger patients.** Millennials and Gen Z actively avoid phone calls. If your business does not offer text-based booking, you are invisible to an entire generation of customers.

We have a full breakdown of the differences in our [AI chatbot vs AI agent comparison](/blog/ai-chatbot-vs-agent/), but the short version is: a text receptionist is a purpose-built AI agent that does intake and booking through messaging — it is not a generic chatbot that hands out links.

## What This Practice Learned (And What You Can Apply)

After the first week, the dental practice made three changes that amplified their results even further:

**1. They put the WhatsApp number everywhere.**
The AI text receptionist lives on their website, their Facebook page, their Google Business Profile, and on a sign at the front desk. Everywhere a potential patient looks, there is a "Text us" button.

**2. They stopped treating web leads differently.**
The website chat widget now feeds into the same AI. A lead from Facebook Ads → landing page → chat widget gets the same instant response as a WhatsApp message. No more forms that sit in an inbox unread.

**3. They started asking for reviews automatically.**
The AI sends a follow-up message 48 hours after each appointment: "How was your visit? Would you mind leaving us a review?" In one week, they collected 12 new Google reviews.

[Getting more customer reviews](/blog/how-to-get-more-customer-reviews-online/) is a topic we have covered before, but having an AI that does it automatically — without the front desk having to remember — is the difference between "we should do that" and actually doing it.
