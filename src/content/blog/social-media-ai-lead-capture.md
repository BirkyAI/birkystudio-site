## The Social Media Leak

Think about what happens when someone sees your post on Instagram or Facebook and wants to buy from you. They tap your profile link. They land on your website or WhatsApp. Then one of three things happens:

**Scenario A:** They land on your website. It looks professional. They scroll, read about your services, and look for a way to contact you. They find a contact form. They fill it out. They wait. Two days later, they have already hired someone else.

**Scenario B:** They send a WhatsApp message at 9 PM with a simple question. "How much does X cost?" Nobody answers until 9 AM the next day. By then, they have messaged three other businesses and received two replies. You lost.

**Scenario C:** They click your Instagram link and land on a page that just shows your phone number and email. No pricing, no booking, no instant answers. They close the tab and keep scrolling.

In every scenario, your social media investment generated interest. And in every scenario, your business failed to capture it.

The fix is not posting more content. It is having a system that answers, qualifies, and books the moment someone reaches out.
