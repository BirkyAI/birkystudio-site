## The problem: a one-person agency still needs a front desk

Birky Studio is small. When I am deep in a build or away from the phone, inquiries still come in. They do not wait. And they arrive in two languages, because I serve clients worldwide.

Before Elliot, the leaks were obvious. Calls at odd hours went to voice mail. A Spanish speaker got an English answer or none at all. Every missed call was a maybe-customer I would never meet. This is the exact pain a voice agent solves, so I put mine to work on myself.

## Why I use him before any client does

Here is a principle I actually hold. I will not sell you something I do not run on my own business. It would be dishonest, and it would mean the tool never gets the hardening it needs.

So Elliot does his job at my front desk before he does anyone else's:

- He picks up calls, day and night, weekdays and weekends
- He answers in English and Spanish
- He takes the caller's details accurately, instead of letting a message get garbled
- He knows when to end the call, so he does not talk a caller's ear off

Every fix I have made to him, and there have been real ones, happened because I heard him fumble on my own line first. Settings for currency, times, and knowing when to say goodbye all came from testing him myself. That is the product you get, the one that was broken in on the owner's own incoming calls.

## What honest usage taught me

Running your own tool changes how you talk about it. I do not make inflated claims, because Elliot answers my own phone and I know exactly what he can and cannot do. If you ask him something our setup does not handle, he says so, and that honesty is worth more than a polished demo.

It also keeps the offer honest. For a small business, forcing a phone call to be answered at all hours is the difference between a lead and a lost one. [The Voice Receptionist](https://birkystudio.com/products/voice-receptionist/) is the one-time setup for that, priced at $550.
