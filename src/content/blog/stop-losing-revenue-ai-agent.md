## The Math You Have Not Done

Let us break down the real numbers. We will use a conservative example based on what we see with our clients at Birky Studio.

Say your business gets 50 inquiries per month across phone calls, WhatsApp messages, website contact forms, and social media DMs. You handle maybe 30 of them during business hours. That leaves 20 inquiries that come in after hours, on weekends, or when you are simply too busy to respond immediately.

Industry data shows that responding to a lead within 5 minutes makes you 10 times more likely to close than responding after 30 minutes. After an hour? The odds drop to nearly zero. Those 20 after-hours leads are not just delayed. They are gone.

Now put a number on it. If your average customer is worth $500 (and for many businesses it is much more), and you are losing 20 leads per month with a conservative 25 percent close rate, that is:

**20 leads x 25% close rate x $500 average value = $2,500 per month in lost revenue.**

That is $30,000 per year. Vanished. Not because your service is bad. Not because your prices are too high. Because nobody answered the phone.
