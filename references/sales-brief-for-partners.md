## The Products (Plain English)

### 🤖 AI Agent Setup — $1,297

**What it does:** A custom AI assistant that lives on the client's computer. It knows their business, their clients, their prices, and their processes. It can:
- Answer emails and draft replies
- Search for properties, products, or info
- Manage their CRM / client database
- Write social media posts, listing descriptions
- Work on WhatsApp, Telegram, and email
- Speak English AND Spanish

**Who needs it:** Real estate agents, consultants, business owners who are overwhelmed by emails, follow-ups, and admin work. People who say *"I don't have enough hours in the day."*

**How to sell it:** *"Imagine having an assistant who knows your business inside-out, works 24/7, never calls in sick, and costs less than a monthly phone bill. That's what this is."*

**What's included:** Setup, knowledge base building (teaching the AI about their business), Notion CRM if they're in real estate, training call. 3 months basic support included.

### 📞 AI Voice Receptionist — $550 setup

**What it does:** An AI that answers phone calls 24/7. Natural voice, bilingual. It:
- Greets callers professionally
- Answers FAQs (hours, pricing, location)
- Qualifies leads (asks questions, captures info)
- Transfers urgent calls to the client's phone
- Takes messages and sends them as text

**Who needs it:** Any business that misses calls — plumbers, dentists, restaurants, clinics, law offices, real estate agents. People who say *"I keep missing calls when I'm busy with a client."*

**How to sell it:** *"How many calls did you miss today? Each one is a lost customer. For a one-time setup fee, your phone is answered 24/7 by a professional AI voice. Never miss a lead again."*

**Extra cost:** Client pays Vapi (the voice provider) ~$30-50/month for actual call minutes. Like paying a phone bill instead of a salary.
